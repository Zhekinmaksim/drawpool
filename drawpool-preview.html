Why OPN

Why this only makes sense on OPN.

DrawPool depends on finalized future-block hashes for fair randomness. On OPN, instant finality means the committed block hash cannot be reorged or re-rolled after the draw commits.

Instant finality

Once the block lands, the winner source is fixed forever.

~1s blocks

The deposit lock between commit and settle stays short enough to feel natural.

OPN integration

This is not generic EVM glue. The randomness design is built around OPN's consensus guarantees.

How the savings draw works
01 / COMMIT
Lock the pool
The contract locks deposits and commits to a future OPN block before the result is known.
02 / FINALIZE
Block finalizes
A few ~1s blocks later OPN finalizes it, and the block hash becomes immutable.
03 / SETTLE
Winner receives yield
The contract harvests yield, selects a balance-weighted winner, and compounds the payout.
